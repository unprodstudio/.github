## 👾 Say **hello** *(or hi, hey, hola, etc.)* to [**UNPROD**](https://x.com/UNPRODSTUDIO)!

<img width="1500" height="674" alt="Productions Promo" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/03a3f38f-a5d9-4758-bbf4-b873e6795381" />

UNPROD is a small indie studio run by Theo ([TheoClouds](https://x.com/theocloudss)) and Aero ([MxAero](https://x.com/ImMxAero)), building mostly story-driven experiences that give players and creators real room to make great content. Founded in 2023, the studio has already reached well over 800k+ eyes, and it's only getting bigger as it carves out its name in the Minecraft creator space.

Right now, the studio is best known for **[SMP Unity](https://x.com/UnityMultiEN)**, the first multilingual factions Minecraft server, which ran for over two years across four seasons of original storylines and live events. SMPU was also where UnityTranslate got its start, which is a free, open-source mod for live voice transcription and translation, something never done before in Minecraft.

*To learn more about UNPROD, take a deeper look at our other projects below!*

## 🛠️ Some of Our Work...

### 🌤️ [SUNFALL](https://x.com/SUNFALLUPDATES) (Creator SMP)

SUNFALL is a new Minecraft SMP with an expansive original story woven around celestials and gods, drawing from tales like the Odyssey, the Iliad, and the Norse Eddas and reimagined into something entirely its own. Backed by 30+ established content creators and hailed as the spiritual successor to SMP Unity, SUNFALL launched on July 11th, 2026, with a full, planned story arc set to unfold over the next six months, all while its suite of custom-built features continues to grow and evolve alongside it.

### 👑 [SMP Unity](https://x.com/UnityMultiEN) (Creator SMP)

SMP Unity was the first multilingual factions SMP, running for over two years before its finale on October 25th, 2025. It featured four full seasons of an ongoing, planned storyline, live events, and countless fully custom mechanics, making it an incredibly successful project that brought serious attention to UNPROD and kicked off the studio as a whole.

### 🌐 [UnityTranslate](https://modrinth.com/mod/unitytranslate) (Public Mod)

UnityTranslate is a free, open-source Minecraft mod built for real-time speech-to-text transcription and translation, displaying messages as speech bubbles or HUD text across more than 44 languages, something never done before in Minecraft. Born out of SMP Unity's need for seamless global communication, it's currently unlisted while undergoing a full rewrite aimed at significantly improving the project, from functionality to customizability.

### 👁️ [Origins SMP](https://modrinth.com/modpack/osmp) (Public Modpack)

Origins SMP is a modpack built around the Origins mod, giving every player a unique set of abilities and drawbacks to build a playstyle around. It layers in performance improvements and extra content on top, delivering a fun, accessible modded experience while keeping Origins firmly at its core.

### 🎙️ [Amended SVC Icons](https://modrinth.com/resourcepack/amendedicons) (Public Resource Pack)

Amended SVC Icons is a resource pack built to refresh all Simple Voice Chat icons with a cleaner, more polished design. It's a small but deliberate visual upgrade, staying true to the original style while making the UI feel sharper and more intentional.

### ⚙️ [Simply Optimized & Up to Date](https://modrinth.com/modpack/simply-optimized-up-to-date) (Public Modpack)

Simply Optimized & Up to Date is a lightweight, performance-focused modpack built to keep the beloved Simply Optimized alive. It updates the original with modern optimization mods and support for the latest Minecraft versions, so players can keep their game running smooth without falling behind.

### 🗿 [Mannequin Essentials](https://modrinth.com/modpack/mannequinessentials) (Public Modpack)

Mannequin Essentials is a lightweight modpack built around the Mannequin mod by Cublem, designed for quick and easy thumbnail creation. It bundles the performance and quality-of-life mods you need for a smooth, no-fuss setup, so you can jump straight into shooting clean, professional-looking thumbnails.

### 🎥 [Flashback Essentials](https://modrinth.com/modpack/flashback-essentials) (Public Modpack)

Flashback Essentials is the sibling modpack to Mannequin Essentials, built around the Flashback mod for quick, easy video creation. It bundles the performance and quality-of-life mods you need for a smooth, no-fuss setup, so you can jump straight into making great cinematics without digging through a mess of configs and crashes.
